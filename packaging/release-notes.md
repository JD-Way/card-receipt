**Downloads** (under Assets below):
- **Card-Receipt-Windows.zip** for Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit)
- **Card-Receipt-Windows7-32bit.zip** for Windows 7 SP1, 8 or 8.1 (32-bit or 64-bit). It runs on an older engine that no longer gets security updates; see its read-me.
- **Card-Receipt-Mac-AppleSilicon.zip** for Macs with an M1 chip or newer
- **Card-Receipt-Mac-Intel.zip** for Macs with an Intel processor (macOS 13 or newer for both Mac versions)

Each zip has a "Read me first" file with install steps. The first time you open the app, Windows or macOS shows a warning because the app isn't signed yet. The read-me explains how to get past it.

**What's new in 1.5**
- **Share list.** A new Export option with just each card's name and its full TCGplayer link. Copy it, share it, email it or save it as a text file.
- **Simple view quantity.** Change quantities with − and + right in simple view.
- **Choose what simple view shows.** Settings > Receipt screen can hide the tabs, saved status, barcode bar, sort menu, quantity, price, Prices and links, Customer and Total. Add this card and the bottom buttons always stay.
- **Barcode bar on or off.** Turn off the "Scan or type a barcode" bar in Settings if you don't use barcodes.
- **Auto save.** Optional, in Settings: the receipt saves itself within 5 minutes of a change and keeps the same receipt number.
- **Customer name.** "Show customer name on receipt" under Customer.
- "Hide price details" is now called **Only show sale price on receipt**, and the Export button no longer has an arrow.

From 1.4: price rounding, the phone layout fix and the Windows 7 version. From 1.3: the Export menu (PDF, PNG, CSV, Excel, email with PDF) and new colors. From 1.2: sorting and drag to rearrange. From 1.1: inventory with barcodes and labels, scan to add, average price, receipt numbers, shop logo and colors, and backups.

Your existing receipts carry over when you install this version.
